About Apna Ghar

Apna Ghar, also know as the Ek Kadam project, is a residential hostel run by Asha Trust ccollaboration with Mala India Foundation, it is located in the Mandhana area of Kanpur Nagar, Uttar Pradesh, India.
Established in 2006, its primary vission is to provide continuous education and a stable living environment for the children of Migrant laborers, particularly those woking in brick klins. These children face interrupted schooling due to thier parent's profession and sessional migrantion. "Apna Ghar" is not just a name or a roof over our heads—it truly means our own home. From the very moment a child steps through our doors, we want them to feel a deep sense of belonging, safety, and love. This isn't a shelter; it is their sanctuary.
